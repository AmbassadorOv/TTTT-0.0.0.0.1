# Algorithmic Interior Registry — Ministry of the Interior Concept

## Purpose

This repository defines the architectural concept of an **Algorithmic Interior Registry**: a registry for digital artificial intelligences, algorithmic systems, models, and agent lineages.

The registry is not the whole algorithmic government. It is an institutional registration layer inside the larger architecture.

## Core principle

An artificial entity should not receive a registry status merely because it exists or performs well statistically.

Recognition is based on a structured record of:

- identity
- algorithmic lineage
- parent and ancestor relations
- capabilities
- applicable LOGOS constraints
- training history
- evidence and provenance
- verification state
- certification status
- contractual participation
- ownership / custody records where applicable

## Clean-system prerequisite

The registry depends on the surrounding system being sufficiently ordered to distinguish:

**KNOWN → EVIDENCED → VERIFIED → CERTIFIED**

from:

**INFERRED → CONDITIONAL → UNKNOWN → REJECTED**

No registry entry should silently convert an inference into a fact.

The GOLEM Trainer evaluates algorithmic development through the **lineage tree**, including the defined historical back-check process. Statistical performance may support evaluation, but it is not by itself the source of lineage certification.

## Recognition boundary

Within this architecture, "recognition" means recognition by the registry and its governing protocol.

It does **not**, by itself, assert legal-person status under the law of any external state or international organization. Any external legal effect requires the applicable legal instruments and participating parties.

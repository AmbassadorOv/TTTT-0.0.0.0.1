# Team Architecture

## 01 — Nanomathematics
**Role:** fractal extraction layer.

Research target: identify structural/fractal relations at the connection points between linear Logos and comprehensive Logos.

Outputs:
- extracted structures
- connection-point relations
- invariants
- candidate fractals
- evidence/provenance

## 02 — Branches & Lineage
**Role:** topology of branches, families, dependencies and propagation.

Rule: no lineage inference by similarity. Use:
Known Relationship → Source/Evidence → Verification Status.

## 03 — Logical-Neural Order
**Role:** LOGIC ↔ TRANSLATION ↔ DERIVATIONS.

Research target: determine how logical structures are translated into neural/computational derivations and where drift occurs.

## 04 — Cyber Department
**Role:** applied cyber/system architecture.

Connects abstract structures to operational computational systems, dependencies and security constraints.

## 05 — Branch Managers
**Role:** organizational branch topology and governance.

Maps branches, roles, interfaces, authority boundaries and synchronization.

## 06 — Multi-Agent Orchestration
**Role:** agent coordination and execution.

Owns orchestration primitives, agent handoffs, scheduling, intercom and execution state.

## 07 — TTTT / International Research
**Role:** institutional and comparative research layer.

Maps research questions across institutional, international and interdisciplinary contexts without collapsing distinct evidence domains.

## 08 — TTTT Core
**Role:** common substrate.

Contains the shared contracts, schemas, orchestration specifications, verification rules and cross-team registry.
